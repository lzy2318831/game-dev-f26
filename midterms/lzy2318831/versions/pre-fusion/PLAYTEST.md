# Human playtest protocol — not yet performed

Have someone else play the working build while the student watches without
helping or selecting moves. Keep the participant anonymous; collect no name,
contact information or personal recording without authorization.

Ask them to start a match, choose direction/power, understand the score/turn,
finish a match and restart. Human vs Human with the student playing the other
seat is possible, but avoid strategic advice; a second baseline/model opponent
lets the student observe more quietly. Record actual behavior, hesitation,
guesses, misunderstandings, ending comprehension and what changed afterward.
