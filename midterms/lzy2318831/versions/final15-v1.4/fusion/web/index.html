THE RULES OF THE TABLE

Every angle
has potential.

  • Point to aim. Press and drag opposite the aiming line. Release to shoot.
  • Pocket an object ball for 100 points.
  • Your cue ball earns 15 per bumper, once per bumper per shot (150 max).
  • Trigger a bumper, then pocket a ball: +50. Each additional pot: +50.
  • A scratch voids all points from the shot. The cue ball is respotted.
  • Side return lanes give +10 each, once per shot. Rounded rails bank the ball back inward.
  • Cross the top Orbit spinner for +25 and 2× subsequent pots in that shot. Combos/device points stay flat.
  • Enter the top-left Skyway for +20 and a powered return into the right reactor. One use per ball per shot.
  • Slings kick upward and inward on contact: cue +10 each. The Vault scoop ejects directly into the reactor: cue +40, once per shot. All device points cap at315 per shot.
  • Six shots each, alternating turns. Clear the table to end early. Higher score wins; equal scores draw.

Right-click or Escape cancels a drag. Physics finishes before the next player acts.
